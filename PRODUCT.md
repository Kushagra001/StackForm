Stackform — a personal agency portfolio for Kushagra Singh Negi that converts visitors into clients by showcasing conversion-focused web development and automation work.

Register: brand

Primary users:
- Founders, CMOs, and business owners at D2C brands, SaaS startups, and local service businesses.

Tone:
- Technically confident, direct, no fluff — like a senior developer talking to a smart client.

Brand constraints and goals:
- Primary accent: #4f6ef7 (electric blue) on near-black #080810.
- Anti-reference: avoid generic "digital agency" tropes — no purple gradients, no teal/coral combos, no neon green, no gradients at all.
- Fonts: Cal Sans (display/headings), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (code/labels).
- Default: dark mode only (no light mode toggle).
- Accessibility target: WCAG AA minimum (contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 for body text).
- Motion: all animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and be disable-able.

Notes:
- Prioritize clarity of case studies and conversion CTA. Keep copy concise; every word must earn its place.
